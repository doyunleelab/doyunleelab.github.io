We are always looking for enthusiastic and self-motivated researchers who are passionate about understanding the neural basis of social behavior.

Our lab provides an intellectually stimulating environment with access to state-of-the-art equipment including two-photon microscopes, Neuropixels probes, and advanced behavioral setups.

Postdoctoral Researcher

We seek a highly motivated postdoc to lead projects on large-scale neural recordings using two-photon imaging or Neuropixels, focused on the neural mechanisms of social recognition and associative social memory.

Preferred Qualifications
  • Animal surgery and behavioral work experience
  • Fluorescence imaging (in vivo or in vitro)
  • In vivo electrophysiology
  • Optogenetics and pharmacogenetics
  • MATLAB or Python programming for data analysis

Graduate Students

We accept graduate students through joint graduate programs with top universities. We seek enthusiastic, self-motivated students with a strong interest in systems neuroscience and social behavior.

Partner Universities
KAIST UNIST GIST Sungkyunkwan University

Student Researchers

We occasionally host undergraduate student researchers. Interested students should contact us directly.

Summer Internships

We participate in IBS summer internship programs for undergraduate students interested in neuroscience research.
