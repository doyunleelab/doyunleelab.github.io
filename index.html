Our Lab

We are an independent research group at the Center for Cognition and Sociality, Institute for Basic Science (IBS), located in Daejeon, Korea.

Established in June 2015, our lab has developed cutting-edge techniques including freely moving whole-cell recordings and large-scale population imaging to probe the neural basis of social behavior.

About the Lab Meet the Team

Doyun Lee, PhD

Senior Research Fellow
Center for Cognition and Sociality, IBS

Dr. Lee received her PhD in Physiology from Seoul National University (2007) and established the lab at IBS in 2015. Her work focuses on the neural mechanisms of social recognition and social motivation.
